TAHA LED Dış Ticaret A.Ş. · Toeled

Toeled, LEDAJANS çatısı altında iç ve dış mekan LED ekran, rental ekran, kontrol kartı ve kurulum hizmeti sunar. Kaliteli ürünü düşük maliyet, hızlı teslimat ve tutarlı servisle İstanbul, Almanya ve Kıbrıs ofislerinden yürütürüz.

In 2007, Toeled launched its P10 product domestically, establishing its industry position. In the following decade or so, Toeled transformed from a single and dual color product to a full-color strategy, and also entered the field of small spacing from full-color products. The development of the R&D sector was unstoppable, and significant achievements were made in new product development, optimization and cost reduction, improvement of product performance, and invention patents.
