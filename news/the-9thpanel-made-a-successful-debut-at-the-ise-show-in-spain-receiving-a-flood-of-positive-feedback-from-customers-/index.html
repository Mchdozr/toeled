Toeled Successfully Concludes ISE 2026

February 7, 2026

ISE 2026, one of the world's leading professional audiovisual exhibitions, has recently concluded successfully in Spain, bringing together industry partners and cutting-edge technologies from around the globe.



At the exhibition, Toeled showcased a comprehensive range of LED display solutions for commercial displays, creative applications, and diverse scenarios. Through dynamic, real-world demonstrations, we engaged in in-depth technical exchanges with global partners and professional visitors, clearly highlighting the products’ strengths in reliability, flexibility, and long-term value. The innovative products and integrated solutions on display received strong interest and positive recognition on site.




This participation further strengthened Toeled’s professional brand presence in the European and global markets. Looking ahead, we remain committed to technological innovation and high-quality service, working closely with partners worldwide to advance the development of the display industry.






We would like to express our heartfelt gratitude to our global customers and partners again for your visit and support throughout the exhibition. The insights shared during our time together provide invaluable direction for Toeled to continue refining display solutions with enduring value. We look forward to seeing you again at our next stop to shape the future of display technology together.
