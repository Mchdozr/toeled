MK Serisi

It is widely used in commercial office spaces, indoor halls, television studios, exhibition halls, cinemas, government departments, conference rooms, entertainment venues, hospitals, performing arts centers, etc., and occupies a prominent position in these fields.




Piksel aralığı (mm) :  MK1.2、MK1.5、MK1.8、MK2、MK2.5

Tanıtım Teknik Özellikler

Tanıtım

Ultra-Light & Slim Build

Weighing under 1.7kg per cabinet, the ultra-light and slim design supports direct magnetic mounting, making it ideal for quick-track projects and weld-free structures.




Dinamik enerji tasarrufu


Powered by DPS dual negative pressure energy-saving technology, it cuts power consumption by over 11% compared to conventional solutions, delivering efficient performance with stable, on-demand power distribution.




 All-in-One Reliable Design


Featuring a cable-free 3-in-1 integrated structure and hard-wired connections, it minimizes failure points while the ultra-light cabinet (under 1.7kg) enables fast, hassle-free installation.
