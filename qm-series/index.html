QM Serisi

The QM series is a newly launched 640*480 aluminum alloy box that is light in weight, extremely thin, multi-purpose in one box, high-precision, and capable of front and rear maintenance. It is suitable for various indoor full-color 320*160 size module to meet the application needs of various indoor places.

Piksel aralığı (mm) :  QM-Pro：QM-Pro-09, QM-Pro-1, QM-Pro-2 QM-E：QM-E


Tanıtım Teknik Özellikler

Tanıtım

Thinner and Lighter

Aluminum alloy cabinet, light weight, ultra-thin, high precision, fully capable of front and back maintenance.

Super Compatibility，One Cabinet for Multipurpose

The size of the cabinet is 640*480mm, which can match 11 types of Qiangli indoor 320*160mm module products. One cabinet, multi-purpose, and the replacement is easier, which really saves you costs.

All New Appearance，Minimalist Design

The cabinet size 640*480, brand-new frame design is full of technology and lines, and the overall appearance is full of modernity and future.

Seamless Splicing, Flat as Mirror

High-precision  aluminum die-casting material, flatness accuracy of the cabinet is less than 0.15mm, seamless splicing, and flat as a mirror.

Humanized Design，Comfortable Handle

The top of the cabinet is equipped with a stretchable rebound handle, which is ergonomically designed, comfortable and durable.

Front and Rear Maintenance

Full front and rear maintenance, wall-mounted, no maintenance access, and extra-large space.
